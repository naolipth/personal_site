Our study looks at whether the global stock markets foresaw Russia's invasion of Ukraine on 24th February 2022 or whether it was a total surprise. Using data from 62 countries we find that **markets started to react to the prospect of war 50 days prior to the invasion** (in early January 2022). The reaction was gradual, with stock prices reflecting the increasing concern as the invasion date got closer. The conclusion is that around two-thirds of the market reaction took place prior to the invasion and only about one-third on the day of the invasion. The war was therefore not a total surprise to the markets and it is clear that **markets can react to major geopolitical risks well in advance of the event actually taking place**.
